Quick answers

Does $1 really make a difference?

On its own, a little. When you ask three friends and they each ask three more, it adds up fast. Your $1 starts that chain.

Is it tax-deductible?

Yes. Guru Garjana Academy is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Giving by Zelle? Email gurugarjana@gmail.com and we'll send your acknowledgement.

Can my employer match it?

Many do, through Benevity. Search for Guru Garjana in your company's giving portal.

Who are you?

A South Florida Dhol-Tasha drumming group, playing since 2022. Every performance raises money for children who need it most. Meet us →
