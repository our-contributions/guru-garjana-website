Our work

Harmony in giving, empowering lives.

We don't run programs. We find people already doing the work, share their story at every show, and fund what they need.

The causes we drum for

We work with those whose vision matches ours

Girls born into hardship

Organizations who believe girls from red-light areas can become powerful agents of change, drawing strength from their backgrounds instead of being held back by them, and who give them the same training, opportunities and resources others take for granted.

Feeding hungry children

Organizations tackling classroom hunger and malnutrition, so children who are eager to learn get a nutritious meal and can stay in school.

Children in foster care

Organizations bringing stability, opportunity and hope to children in the foster care system through education, emotional support and enrichment programs.

Protecting children from abuse

Organizations giving adults and communities the knowledge and skills to keep children safe, through education, advocacy and training.

Animals in need

Our compassion reaches abandoned, neglected and at-risk pets too. We support animal welfare groups that give them shelter, food and love.
