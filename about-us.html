About us

Why we drum.

We're a South Florida Dhol-Tasha drumming academy, playing since 2022. Our music moves crowds, and it helps change the lives of children born into hardship, especially girls.

Our vision

A world where every child gets to compose their own life's melody.

We picture children, especially girls from red-light areas, safe, in school and guided by mentors, growing into confident adults who choose their own tune.

Our mission

Turning rhythm into real change.

Music has the power to unite, inspire and transform lives.

Our drums get people's attention. Then we ask them to help. Every performance raises money for children who need it most.

Money is only part of it. We want these children to have schooling, mentors and life skills, so they can rise above hard beginnings.

Every child deserves the chance to find their own rhythm. We're here to play our part.
