One beat at a time

A beautiful Saturday morning, a beat for a cause

A beautiful Saturday morning. Rushing to a serene, shady patch of a park. Meeting like-minded folks who believe in music, rhythm, solidarity and, most importantly, the cause, the goal, the vision.

Unloading the drums and tightening the tasha under the crisp Florida sun, in a lush green field.

Bringing the big drums, the dhol, and the smaller but powerful melody driver, the tasha, from halfway across the world was costly and full of challenges. But it was worth every effort and every penny.

Alongside these two leads we have two more core instruments: the zanj and the lezim. The zanj acts like a metronome, while the lezim brings dance and rhythm together, creating a truly wholesome experience.

The many benefits of drumming

Playing any musical instrument has huge physical and mental benefits, especially traditional instruments like the dhol, tasha, zanj and lezim. Here's why.

Mental and emotional benefits

  • Stress relief. Drumming helps you forget your worries. It calms the mind and works like a moving meditation.
  • Mood booster. Percussion releases endorphins, the feel-good hormones, so you feel lighter and happier.
  • Focus and concentration. Playing in a group takes sharp attention and rhythm, which gives your brain a workout.
  • Brain health. Drumming may boost BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor), a molecule that supports learning, memory and neuroplasticity.

Physical benefits

  • A great workout. Drumming improves heart health, stamina and muscle tone.
  • Better coordination. It sharpens motor skills and hand-eye coordination.
  • Faster reflexes. Your reaction times improve the more you drum.

Musical and social benefits

  • Rhythm and timing. Essential for every musician.
  • Team spirit. These instruments sync beautifully when played together, teaching collaboration and harmony.
  • Creativity. Freestyling builds imagination and confidence.
  • Empathy and adaptability. Live performances teach us to read the room and work with others.

Beyond music: a mind, body and spirit journey

Drumming with the dhol, tasha, zanj and lezim is not just music. It's:

  • A journey into an ancient tradition
  • A boost to your physical and mental wellbeing
  • A path to human connection and healing

It's about spreading humanity

Yes, you read that right. In today's divided world, we drum to unite.

We drum so a mother doesn't put her kids to bed hungry.
We drum for a father who dreams of his daughter's future.
We drum so underserved kids get food, clothes and education.
We drum to support non-profits that heal and rebuild.

At Guru Garjana, we don't just perform. We drum with purpose. We drum for a cause.
